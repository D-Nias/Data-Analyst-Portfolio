Business question

Where is the existing EV population concentrated, and which vehicle types dominate the current registry? A charging-network team could use this as one input when deciding where to investigate demand.

Recommendation: Start deeper market research in King, Snohomish, and Pierce counties, then normalize by population, total vehicle fleet, charger availability, and travel patterns before choosing sites.

What would justify a closer investment screen?

The registry gives a starting point. The next step needs comparable denominators and supply data before ranking counties or locations.

MeasureDefinitionData needed and use
EV adoption contextRegistered EVs ÷ registered passenger vehicles × 1,000Use a same-date fleet denominator to distinguish county size from EV share.
Public charging supplyPublic ports ÷ registered EVs × 1,000, split by Level 2 and DC fast chargingAdd a dated charger inventory. Port counts do not measure utilization, uptime, or home charging.
Candidate-area screenEVs and traffic within a defined drive-time area, plus site and grid feasibilityRequires finer geography, traffic counts, candidate parcels, and utility capacity; county totals cannot select a site.

Align the vehicle and charger snapshots in time and geography. Advance counties only for neighborhood-level research; this registry alone does not establish unmet demand or predict station performance.

Checks and limitations

The downloaded file contains 299,705 records. I filtered to State = WA, leaving 298,916 records and the same number of distinct DOL vehicle IDs. This is a current registry snapshot. Model year is a vehicle attribute, so model-year counts should not be labeled annual sales or new registrations.
